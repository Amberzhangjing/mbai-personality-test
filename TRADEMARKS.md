# MBAI brand policy

The source code and assessment content are openly licensed, but the **MBAI** name and official brand identifiers remain source identifiers of the official project.

You may truthfully state that your work is based on or compatible with MBAI. You may not imply that a modified test, hosted copy, product, or service is official, endorsed, certified, or maintained by the MBAI project without written permission.

Forks that materially change questions, scoring, result definitions, or presentation should use a distinct product name and state that they are derived from MBAI.

This policy does not limit nominative or otherwise lawful references to the MBAI project.
