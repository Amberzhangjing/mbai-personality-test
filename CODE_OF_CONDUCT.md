# Code of Conduct

MBAI is a playful project, but collaboration should remain respectful.

- Critique ideas and wording, not people or identities.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or sexualized attention.
- Assume good faith while being specific about evidence and impact.
- Respect privacy and do not post another person's test answers without permission.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and interactions that violate these expectations.
